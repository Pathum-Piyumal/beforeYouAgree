Before You Agree: privacy and limitations

Analysis starts after you review the text, select consent and press Analyze. Only the reviewed sections and source URLs are sent to your configured backend. With hosted AI enabled, the backend forwards them to OpenAI with response storage disabled. Provider-side processing and retention depend on the operator's account and policies. Mock mode makes no external AI requests.

Remove personal account details, payment information, passwords and confidential text before analysis. Extraction excludes hidden and editable fields but can still include personal details written in ordinary paragraphs. Source URLs can also contain sensitive query values.

Guest requests create no saved database records. A five-minute browser session cache retains structured reports and evidence excerpts. An explicit account save retains source metadata, findings, quoted evidence and commitments. Raw submitted section bodies are not stored in the database or intentionally logged.

Saved data remains until report or account deletion. Deletion removes the live database records; backup retention is controlled by the service operator. Account deletion requires your current password and invalidates all account sessions.

Account tokens use Chrome session storage, or tab-scoped sessionStorage in the standalone dashboard. Content scripts never receive these tokens. Browser-local reminders stay on this device independently of your account and are not removed by deleting an account or saved report. Account reminders are stored on the backend and cascade with their reports.

Reminders are in-app notices. No email, SMS or desktop notifications are sent. Due status means a reminder is available for review; only your acknowledgement marks it reviewed. Do not rely on this application as your only way to meet an important deadline.

The extension uses activeTab, storage and alarms. Its content script responds to inspection requests and does not automatically extract or upload browsing content. Linked policies are opened separately and never fetched automatically.

This is decision support, not legal advice. Scores are versioned heuristics, not probabilities. Missing findings, low scores and partial evidence do not establish that an agreement is safe or enforceable.
